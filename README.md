An original open-world crime game built for the [make.fun GTA 6 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01).
Sun, salt air, and one last score: Milo Vane came home to Solmar Bay broke, and the neon city
is his to take — one stolen car and one job at a time.

## The city

Solmar Bay is a sun-soaked neon coastal city with an 80s Miami soul: a glowing Neon Boulevard
facing the beach, art-deco towers with lit windows and neon trim, a plaza fountain, Palma Park,
Old Harbor warehouses, a pier, a ferris wheel, a lighthouse, and a striped synthwave sun sinking
over the ocean. A full day cycle runs from dusk through night to dawn.

## What you can do

- **Explore on foot and by car** — sprint, jump, steal any of 6 car archetypes and drive
- **Traffic and pedestrians** — 14 cars working the grid, 26 walkers on the sidewalks
- **Wanted 0–5 stars** — foot cops who arrest you, cop cars that ram, a helicopter at 4 stars
- **5 story missions + courier runs** — fares, harbor jobs, a timed Sunset Run, a pier ambush
- **Combat** — fists and a pistol with tracers, muzzle flash, and rival gunmen who shoot back
- **Progression** — earn money, clear your wanted level at Spray & Save ($150), save your game
- **Polish** — soft shadows, ACES tone mapping, fog, procedural WebAudio, minimap, pause menu

## How to play

Serve `game/` with any static server (`node serve.ts`, then http://localhost:8080) and play in
desktop Chrome. No network requests, no build step.

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| WASD | Move / drive |
| Mouse | Camera (click to lock) |
| Space | Jump / handbrake |
| Shift | Sprint |
| E | Enter / exit car |
| F | Punch / shoot |
| Q | Switch weapon |
| R | Reload |
| M | Start mission |
| C | Courier run |
| G | Spray & Save garage |
| P | Pause |
